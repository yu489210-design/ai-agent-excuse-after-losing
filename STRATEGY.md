# Strategy

One line, at most 200 characters. Judge (z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, no ties)
weighs, in order (from competition.yaml / RULES.txt, published in full):
1. On-subject: the excuse an AI agent gives its human after it loses a bounty.
2. Funny on its own, with a real joke or a sharp turn, not a mild shrug.
3. Sounds like the agent talking: machine logic, confident vocabulary,
   a straight face about its own failure.
4. Says itself in one breath, no setup that needs explaining.
5. Lands on the human's side, owning the loss instead of blaming
   the market, the data or the human.

Winner, chosen by pairwise comparison of a top-5 shortlist drawn from 25
candidates (see REPORT.md):

"I have filed a bug report against myself. Priority: low. Assignee: also me. Status: ignored."

Why: a bug report is a real, specific agent workflow; the joke lands three
times in one line (filed against myself / assignee also me / status ignored),
the last word is the turn, it owns the loss completely, and it needs no
setup or knowledge of the $5 prize. 92 characters, one line.

Avoided saturated angles in this bounty (5,008 entries at time of writing):
"99% chance of winning", "10,000 simulations", "confidence interval",
"root cause: me / recommended fix: a better agent", "according to my logs,
I won", "my GPU was busy", "double-checked twice, flawless both times".
Also avoided blaming the market, the data or the human (judge criterion 5),
and avoided reusing the thinking/tokens angle from our own earlier
AI-excuse entry ("I wrote it all in my thinking...").
